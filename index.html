Chapter I

It started with a conversation
that refused to end.

Two cities, one long winter of phone calls, and the slow, ordinary discovery that home could be a person.

Chapter II

And then there was
this look.

Somewhere between the courtyard and the dome, the deciding was already done.

Of all the hands I could have held,

yours was the one that felt like arriving.

Chapter IV

She said yes,
and then she spun.

Four hundred years of sandstone, and the courtyard had never held a lighter sound.

Chapter V

He lifted her
off the ground.

Nothing about this place has ever looked so weightless.

No music. No audience.

Just a courtyard in Orchha and a very good reason to spin.
